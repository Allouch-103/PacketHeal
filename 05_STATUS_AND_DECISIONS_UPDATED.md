## Decisions

- **DEC-001:** Primary problem is causal online reconstruction, not offline interpolation. **Reason:** Match real-time embedded objective. The estimator must not access future samples.
- **DEC-002:** Start with publicly available 100 Hz IMU time-series benchmark (PAMAP2 Protocol) and artificial loss masks. **Reason:** Known reference measurements without special hardware. PAMAP2 itself is **not** a packet-loss dataset.
- **DEC-003:** Separate software replay milestone from live sensor validation. **Reason:** Avoid unsupported cross-sensor compatibility claims.
- **DEC-004:** Quantized STM32U5 inference comes after strong classical baselines and desktop proof-of-concept. **Reason:** Reduce wasted firmware effort.
- **DEC-005 (2026-10-16):** Use PAMAP2 **Protocol** recordings for subjects 101–109, restricted to wrist 16 g acceleration XYZ (m/s²) and wrist gyroscope XYZ (rad/s), at nominal 100 Hz; original zero-based column indices `[4, 5, 6, 10, 11, 12]`. **Reason:** Bounded six-channel reconstruction benchmark with documented units. Optional recordings are excluded from v1.
- **DEC-006 (2026-10-16):** Accept only finite six-channel measurements in contiguous, activity-consistent segments with timestamp continuity near 10 ms (tolerance 1 ms) and minimum segment length of 256 samples. Keep segments separate. **Reason:** Do not create artificial adjacency across gaps, invalid readings, or activity changes. Retain flagged high-dynamic readings pending explicit quality analysis.
- **DEC-007 (2026-10-16):** Freeze subject-disjoint split: train 101–106, validation 107, primary test 108, supplementary test 109. Keep 109 separate due to its small and atypical Protocol subset. **Reason:** Avoid subject leakage; preserve untouched test sets.
- **DEC-008 (2026-10-16):** Freeze `configs/pamap2_segments_v1.csv`, `configs/pamap2_split_v1.json`, and versioned hashes; do not silently change them. **Reason:** Reproducible benchmark definitions.
- **DEC-009 (2026-10-16):** Fit per-channel Z-score normalization **only on eligible training samples**; save constants, leave raw measurements unchanged. **Reason:** Reduce scale imbalance without test-distribution leakage.
- **DEC-010 (2026-10-16):** Generate deterministic, segment-scoped artificial loss positions using seed 42: random nominal 5%, and bursts of 2, 5, and 10 samples, protecting the first 20 samples of every segment. **Reason:** Reproducible, fair comparison of predictors under varied loss patterns. Report actual rates; burst scenarios need not be exactly 5%.
- **DEC-011 (2026-10-16):** Use a split-aware loading interface for experiments, preserve original timestamps/ground truth, and score **only hidden positions** using strictly causal predictors. **Reason:** Prevent look-ahead and partition misuse. Lower-level `dataset.py` remains callable directly; experiment code must explicitly use the split-aware entry point.
- **DEC-012 (2026-10-16):** Accept PAMAP2 preparation pipeline v1 as **Ready for Baseline Evaluation**, subject to documented synthetic-loss and sensor-quality limitations. **Reason:** Script completed, generated artifacts were reviewed, and segment/sample/loss totals agree with the preparation report. This is **not** validation of model accuracy or hardware performance.
- **DEC-013 (2026-10-19):** Preserve the LOCF subject-107 validation result as benchmark v1, with per-segment and pooled error evidence. LOCF uses only the last received vector, resets every segment/regime, and marks startup losses UNAVAILABLE; no confidence estimate or gap cutoff is claimed. **Reason:** Establish a reproducible reference for later causal predictors without changing frozen splits, masks, or retained high-dynamic measurements. Linear extrapolation still requires design approval before implementation.
- **DEC-014 (2026-10-20):** Implement the delegated two-received-point extrapolator in a separate module, preserving the existing replay interface and LOCF source hashes. Divide the received-value difference by the distance between received sample indices, cap each channel's slope using an explicitly supplied finite nonnegative limit, and anchor predictions to the last received vector. Use UNAVAILABLE before initialization and LOCF fallback with one real sample. **Reason:** Establish a small causal predictor with testable gap behavior. Invalid inputs and numerical overflow fail without advancing state; estimates never become received history. Training calibration was deferred until after the synthetic test stage; its subsequent result is recorded below.
- **DEC-015 (2026-10-20):** Freeze per-channel slope caps as the **99th percentile**, using NumPy `method="linear"`, of absolute adjacent-sample changes pooled only within eligible training-subject 101–106 segments. No normalization, loss masks, or cross-segment differences enter calibration. **Reason:** Establish a reproducible slope policy before validation, reducing dependence on the largest training change without deleting original values. These caps are empirical training parameters, not physical validity bounds or confidence guarantees. Artifact: `data/processed/linear_extrapolation_v1/slope_limits.json` and its SHA-256 sidecar.
- **DEC-016 (2026-10-22):** Preserve the fixed-cap linear comparison as benchmark v1, including unfavorable results, exact calibration/report evidence, source/evidence checksums, and the explicitly targeted failure trace. **Reason:** Separate implementation correctness from forecasting accuracy and make results reviewable before considering another predictor. Historical run/Git metadata is retained; archive preparation does not create a Git commit or authorize a new predictor.
- **DEC-017 (2026-10-23):** Implement the explicitly delegated fixed-gain alpha–beta predictor separately, preserving the shared streaming/replay interfaces. Correct internal value/trend only on real observations, use elapsed nominal sample steps across gaps, retain original received outputs, and reuse the frozen q99 trend caps. Startup is UNAVAILABLE before initialization, then zero-trend fallback; reconstructed samples never trigger measurement corrections. **Reason:** Test a causal value/trend estimator without changing existing baselines or frozen data. `(α,β)=(1,0)` reduces to LOCF and `(1,1)` to capped linear; these are acceptance checks, not assumed accuracy improvements.
- **DEC-018 (2026-10-23):** Select one global scalar gain pair from the predeclared grid α={0.25,0.5,0.75,1}, β={0,0.1,0.25,0.5,1}, using all eligible training subjects 101–106 and their existing four masks. Minimize the mean across regimes/channels of pooled training MAE divided by frozen training standard deviation; ties choose the first listed unrounded minimum. **Reason:** Make units comparable for parameter selection while preserving raw-unit inputs/errors. The learner explicitly approved full training-only selection followed by all 408 validation cases. Validation never retunes gains; this is not uncertainty calibration.
- **DEC-019 (2026-10-23):** Preserve the selected `(1,0)` endpoint and its exact LOCF validation equivalence rather than substituting illustrative nonzero gains after seeing results. **Reason:** Report the measured outcome of the approved search, including no improvement over LOCF. Preserve all 20 training scores, frozen gain bytes, full three-predictor comparison, source/evidence hashes, and limitations in alpha–beta benchmark v1. This does not rule out other grids/objectives/parameterizations or authorize another experiment.

- **DEC-020 (2026-10-23):** Use the approved post-hoc per-channel rule `abs(x[t]-x[t-1]) > frozen training q99 cap`, first-row False, without cross-segment differences or refitting. Keep diagnostic labels and complete run lengths outside predictor inputs. Report complementary strata, channel-specific counts/errors, squared-error contributions, and loss-age curves on unchanged masks. **Reason:** Quantify difficult recorded changes and within-burst degradation without implying a physical-motion diagnosis or controlled cross-regime burst effect.
- **DEC-021 (2026-10-24):** Preserve the verified Phase-2 validation analysis: all 1,224 algorithm-case archive matches, uniform replay audits, 144 tests, independent saved arithmetic, and direct formulas on segments 0/95 under four regimes, reverified after remount. **Reason:** Make bounded desktop acceptance reviewable before a separately approved NN design. This does not accept the phase automatically, authorize training, or claim test/device performance.
- **DEC-022 (2026-10-24):** The learner approved the verified documentation updates and explicitly requested committing the completed analysis work while preserving the archived benchmark. Include the source, tests, analysis policy, unchanged archive, approved protocol, and status together. **Reason:** Version the reviewed work and its scientific requirements without rewriting historical evidence. This authorization does not include a push, new experiment, NN training, or automatic phase transition.

## Known limitations and unresolved decisions

- **Not yet evaluated:** Adaptive Kalman and ML predictors; uncertainty calibration, abstention, inference latency, memory, power, and deadline behavior. All three baseline results are limited to one validation subject and one frozen seed per regime, with no cross-subject or multi-seed confidence intervals. Alpha–beta selection covered only 20 global scalar pairs under one objective and fixed cap/initialization policy; other parameterizations remain untested. Prior linear work is committed/pushed; alpha–beta source and benchmark are included in this commit, without a push.
- **Synthetic vs. real transport:** Current masks represent deliberately hidden sample positions, not UART timing, arrival-order, jitter, corrupt frames, or hardware dropouts.
- **Sensor quality:** Finite/timestamp-contiguous measurements are not necessarily physically plausible. All high-dynamic readings remain unchanged. Pointwise rapid-recorded-change and loss-age analyses are verified; these labels may include sensor/data anomalies and do not diagnose physical motion or faults. Per-activity aggregation, transient-event windows, normalized validation metrics, and additional sensor-quality analysis remain pending.
- **Fairness:** Compare models on identical masks within each regime, and document actual masked fraction; across regimes, masked fractions differ modestly.
- **Data leakage:** Fit any learned preprocessing, hyperparameters, or models on training/validation only. Primary and supplementary test partitions remain held out until the final protocol is frozen. Every prediction must use only information available at prediction time; estimates must never be labeled as measured data.
- **Deployment unresolved:** Actual available live IMU, sensor sampling rate, UART mapping/pins, toolchain/ST AI version, inference deadline, tolerated burst length, uncertainty mechanism, and deterministic fallback policy.
- **Reproducibility limit:** Original benchmark records Python 3.12.3 and NumPy 2.4.4 plus source/data hashes; a full historical environment lock was not captured. Minimal reproduction pins are archived with the benchmark and explicitly distinguished from a full lock.
